Chandrayaan 3


Chandrayaan-3 was launched by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) to demonstrate end-to-end capability in safe landing and roving on the lunar surface, following the partial failure of Chandrayaan-2. The mission aimed to explore the lunar south pole, a region of high scientific interest due to the presence of water ice and unique geological features, which could provide insights into the Moon’s history and potential resources for future missions
